## 3. impact vs busyness
- Simon Sinek. *Start With Why* (TED talk / book)
- Shreyas Doshi. Lenny ep. Jack Dorsey cutting through metrics theater: "how does this make our users love Twitter more?... we never really talked about that because we were so engrossed in all the other stuff."
- Fareed Mosavat. Lenny ep. "what you do multiplied by your ability to show that work and communicate it... that's the impact. not just on customers, but on the organization."
- Evan Lapointe. Lenny ep. "hamsters have bias to action. they turn the wheel hard and go nowhere. you want bias to impact, not bias to action." (cross-link from dim 7)

## 9. self-awareness + reflection
- Carol Dweck. *Mindset* (growth mindset)
- therapy (personal)
- Jonny Miller. Lenny ep (Nervous System Mastery). "feather brick dump truck": notice the feather (subtle early signal) before the dump truck (crisis). maps to noticing your own recurring shapes.
- Claire Hughes Johnson. *Scaling People* / Lenny ep. "the book starts with you... self-awareness is the first operating principle... the most fundamental thing you need to crack." backs marshall's "unlock for every other dimension" claim. her "operating principles / operating manual" framing also cross-links to dim 6.
- outside-corpus candidates to consider: Tasha Eurich *Insight* (definitive self-awareness research, internal vs external SA); Marshall Goldsmith *What Got You Here Won't Get You There* (triggers + behavioral patterns); Daniel Goleman *Emotional Intelligence*.

## 10. pain
- Brené Brown. on vulnerability and the armor people put on. *Daring Greatly* (armored vs daring leadership).
- Carole Robin. *Connect* + Lenny ep. DOUBLE-ANCHOR with dim 4. two concepts for "name it without festering":
  - the **15% rule**: comfort zone in the middle, danger zone outside, growth is the 15% stretch beyond comfort.
  - **appropriate disclosure**: "i have never needed you all more" (calibrated naming) vs "i'm feeling crappy, maybe i shouldn't be VP" (unloading). gives the line between productive naming (builder) and venting/festering (naysayer).
- Susan David. *Emotional Agility* (TED + book). STRONGEST structural match found: her show-up / step-out / walk-your-why / move-on framework basically is marshall's notice then name then decide. NOT in Lenny corpus, outside suggestion.
- Bessel van der Kolk. *The Body Keeps the Score* (unprocessed pain stays active in the body).
- "hurt people hurt people" (marshall quotes it in the post). **TODO origin**: commonly traced to Sandra D. Wilson, popularized by Rick Warren. verify before attributing.
- ~~Ben Williams~~. false positive (Snyk *software* vulnerabilities, not emotional).
