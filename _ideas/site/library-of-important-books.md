# library of important books

books on engineering management, leadership, and adjacent areas that have been
highly influential to me. inspired by lenny's "must-reads" list — a curated
shelf, not a dump.

status: draft list. trim to what i've actually read + what landed. add what's
missing. eventually a `/library` (or `/books`) page on the site.

## engineering management

- **the manager's path** — camille fournier. tech ic → manager → exec ladder. field guide for each rung.
- **an elegant puzzle** — will larson. systems thinking applied to org design, scaling teams, eng strategy.
- **staff engineer** — will larson. the ic track past senior. what "staff+" actually means.
- **the making of a manager** — julie zhuo. first-time manager, honest and concrete.
- **resilient management** — lara hogan. teams, 1:1s, feedback, sponsorship.
- **become an effective software engineering manager** — james stanier. nuts-and-bolts onboarding to the job.

## leadership / orgs

- **high output management** — andy grove. output = team leverage. the root text most others cite.
- **the hard thing about hard things** — ben horowitz. decisions with no good options. wartime ceo.
- **multipliers** — liz wiseman. leaders who amplify vs. drain the people around them.
- **turn the ship around!** — david marquet. push authority to the info, not info to authority.
- **leaders eat last** / **start with why** — simon sinek. trust, safety, purpose.
- **radical candor** — kim scott. care personally + challenge directly.
- **the five dysfunctions of a team** — patrick lencioni. trust → conflict → commitment → accountability → results.

## systems / process / craft

- **accelerate** — forsgren/humble/kim. dora metrics. what actually predicts delivery performance.
- **the phoenix project** — gene kim. devops as a novel. flow, wip, constraints.
- **team topologies** — skelton/pais. stream-aligned/platform/enabling teams. conway's law on purpose.
- **the mythical man-month** — brooks. adding people to a late project makes it later. still true.
- **peopleware** — demarco/lister. productivity is sociological, not technical.

## sports analytics

- **moneyball** - michael lewis. market inefficiency + data over scouting gut. the origin story.
- **the book: playing the percentages in baseball** - tango/lichtman/dolphin. the sabermetrics bible. run expectancy, leverage, wpa.
- **astroball** - ben reiter. moneyball 2.0, blending stats + scouting + culture.
- **the signal and the noise** - nate silver. forecasting, probability, why most predictions fail.
- **basketball on paper** - dean oliver. four factors. founding text of nba analytics.
- **the midrange theory** - seth partnow. modern nba analytics, decision-making under uncertainty.
- **the numbers game** - anderson/sally. soccer analytics. why the boring stats win.
- **soccernomics** - kuper/szymanski. economics + data applied to football.

## decision making / cognitive science

- **thinking, fast and slow** - kahneman. two systems. your biases, named. (also the root text here.)
- **superforecasting** - tetlock/gardner. what makes some people genuinely good at prediction.
- **thinking in bets** - annie duke. decide on process, not outcome. resulting is the trap.
- **how to decide** - annie duke. practical toolkit version of the above.
- **noise** - kahneman/sibony/sunstein. variability in judgment, the bias nobody measures.
- **the art of thinking clearly** - rolf dobelli. 99 cognitive errors, one per chapter.
- **predictably irrational** - dan ariely. systematic, repeatable irrationality.
- **algorithms to live by** - christian/griffiths. cs solutions mapped onto human decisions.
- **how to measure anything** - douglas hubbard. quantify the "intangible" before you decide.
- **the righteous mind** - jonathan haidt. moral reasoning is mostly post-hoc justification.

## thinking / decisions / self

- **thinking in systems** — donella meadows. stocks, flows, leverage points.
- **the effective executive** — drucker. manage your time and contribution, not activity.
- **thinking, fast and slow** — kahneman. two systems. your biases, named.
- **deep work** — cal newport. focus as the scarce skill.
- **drive** — daniel pink. autonomy, mastery, purpose > carrots/sticks.
