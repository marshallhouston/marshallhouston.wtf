03writing — where you leak

gap · the big one

the funnel doesn't drain

~80 kernels + ~65 idea docs → 28 posts. fermentation is your method, but a lot of it is just sitting. "let it ferment" can be a polite word for "didn't ship." you wrote a whole post about ship-it-earlier-than-you-want and the backlog says you don't always take your own medicine.

fix: a quit-condition on ideas. if a kernel hasn't sprouted in N revisits, kill it or ship it ugly. you literally have a "quit-conditions" kernel — sprout it.
gap

versionitis

meb v0, v1, v2, v3, v4. writing-process v2. multiple live drafts of the same post sitting side by side. the parallel-everything instinct (same one driving 157 preach-hub worktrees) means revisions branch instead of converge. iteration without a merge step.

fix: name a canonical, delete the rest. one source of truth per post.
gap

scaffolding left standing

posts are full of <!-- TODO: photo of notebook -->, "screenshots here", "screen recording of…". the structural intent is there; the assets never land. the published artifact carries the ghost of unfinished.

fix: a pre-publish hook that greps for TODO/screenshot in posts without draft:true and blocks the flip.
gap

jargon density vs. the new reader

your coined words are a strength until a post assumes four of them at once. bff-fractal-ptvm-lowerchaos with no on-ramp reads as in-group code to someone arriving cold. the satire risk too: "unpromptable" is parody, but stripped of context it can read as the LinkedIn slop it's mocking.

fix: one plain-language anchor sentence per coined term, first use. let the weird earn its place.

06claude code — where you leak

gap · loudest signal

you don't use the graph you built

Bash 20,628 and Read 12,629 against tokensave 88. you have a global CLAUDE.md rule that mandates tokensave over Explore agents and raw greps for code research — and the numbers say it's barely touched. you built the better tool and kept reaching for grep. classic friction-named-but-not-fixed.

fix: a PreToolUse hook that intercepts grep/find-on-code and suggests tokensave. make the rule mechanical, not aspirational.
gap

worktree sprawl = context fragmentation

157 preach-hub sessions across dozens of worktrees (auth-verify-ttl, go-deeper-drain, og-image-version, parables…). same versionitis as the writing: many parallel half-states, unclear which merged. branches are cheap to open, expensive to leave open.

fix: a stale-worktree GC in /next — list branches with no commit in N sessions, offer kill/ship.
gap

Read-heavy re-orientation

12.6k Reads vs 4.8k Edits — a ~2.6:1 look-to-change ratio. some is healthy caution; a lot is re-reading the same files across fragmented sessions because context got dropped. the burst-work pattern means you re-pay the orientation cost every spurt.

fix: lean on /handoff + tokensave session-recall at spurt boundaries so the next burst starts warm.
gap

discipline outsourced to hooks (fragile)

no-time-estimates, no-em-dash, memory-block, inclusive-language — all enforced by hooks. powerful, but it means the behaviors aren't internalized; they live in one repo's config. on a fresh machine or a non-hooked context, the guardrails vanish. you've engineered around the habit instead of building it.

fix: fine for now — but know the hooks ARE the discipline. keep them in version control and treat their loss as a real risk.
